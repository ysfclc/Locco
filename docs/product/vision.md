# Vision

Locco is a personal, local-first mobile intelligence and action environment. It connects information and capabilities fragmented across websites, apps, files, messages, maps, devices and workflows. It preserves original evidence and provenance, builds a navigable model of entities, claims and relationships, supports bounded on-device intelligence, and composes controlled actions from reusable capabilities.

Locco includes a **proper native browser** that is useful without AI: navigation, tabs, account/session profiles, history, bookmarks, downloads and site controls. Its browser also exposes bounded, structured DOM observations that local AI can stitch into evidence-backed schemas, records and reusable semantic structures. The **Model Hub** is a rich ecosystem for discovering, comparing, verifying, installing and running compatible language, embedding, reranking, vision, speech, audio, image and multimodal models—only where runtime support and actual device measurements justify availability.

Locco is not defined by one vertical or a library of hardcoded business workflows. A general-purpose compiler turns goals into typed requirements and assembles versioned capabilities, knowledge, tools, procedures, constraints and evaluators. Deterministic domain rules retain authority over identity, permissions, approvals, state and verification; models help interpret and propose, but cannot grant authority or declare an external action successful.

It is local-first and native on Android, with Rust owning domain truth and replaceable platform/runtime adapters. No hosted service, paid model or cloud provider is mandatory. A future iOS shell is gated and must share the same domain contracts. Memo-1 and Memo-2 are inspiration and audit references, not templates to copy.

Research, personal knowledge, operations, automation, development, sourcing and location intelligence are possible expressions of the same engine.

**Acquire → preserve → understand → connect → reason → decide → act → verify → learn.** The user remains the authority.
